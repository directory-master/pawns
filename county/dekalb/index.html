About pawn shops in DeKalb County

DeKalb County has 91 pawn shops and valuables buyers across Chamblee, Decatur, Doraville, Lithonia and Stone Mountain. All operate under Georgia's pawnbroker law: charges capped at 25 percent a month for the first 90 days, a written pawn ticket, and ID recorded on every transaction.
