About pawn shops in Gwinnett County

Gwinnett County has 141 pawn shops and valuables buyers across Buford, Lawrenceville, Lilburn, Norcross and Snellville. All operate under Georgia's pawnbroker law: charges capped at 25 percent a month for the first 90 days, a written pawn ticket, and ID recorded on every transaction.
